The Script

Hey [Name], it's Chris. I know I'm catching you out of the blue here, but can I grab 20 seconds to tell you why I called, and you tell me if it's relevant?
[wait for yes]
I actually pulled up your Google listing before calling — you've got [X] reviews at [Y] stars, last one was [Z] ago, so people clearly love what you do.

But you're not showing up in the top 3 map results for "[service] near [city]" — [competitor] is ranking above you right now, and that's where 70% of the clicks go. Those are real customers going to them instead of you every day.

My company — Crestline Commerce — we fix exactly that to make your company more visible to customers in your area.
Quick question — are you actively trying to grow through Google right now, or is most of your business word-of-mouth/repeat customers?
[listen — this tells you if they're a fit and gets them talking]
I can show you exactly where you're losing ground and what fixing it looks like — free, no obligation. I've got time tomorrow at [4 or 4:30]. Which time works?
